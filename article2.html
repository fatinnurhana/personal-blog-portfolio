Essay 2: Version Control as a Digital Narrative and Living Resume

GitHub is far grander than a simple storage for scripts; it is a repository of a developer's growth, mistakes, and triumphs over code.

Every commit message writes a line in our digital diary. It shows how we adapt, how we structure our logical thinking, and functions as a living, breathing resume that proves our real-world problem-solving capabilities to the world.
