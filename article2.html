Essay 2: Version Control as a Digital Narrative and Living Resume

GitHub is far grander than a simple storage for scripts; it is a repository of a developer’s growth and storytelling. Through deliberate, meaningful commits, we expose our problem-solving thought processes, clean code hygiene, and readiness for modern agile collaboration.

When an employer looks at your GitHub profile, they aren't just looking at the final product. They are tracking your timeline—how you branch, how you resolve code conflicts, and how adaptively you deploy versions over time. It truly acts as a living resume that speaks louder than static text.
