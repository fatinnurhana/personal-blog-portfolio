Spoopy Games That'll Make Every Night An Exciting Night

Get ready to wear your ghoulishly fashionable pants in the spirit of Halloween. Horror games, or affectionately dubbed as "spoopy" games, have carved a massive niche in the modern interactive entertainment ecosystem. But what exactly makes them so addictive?

Unlike traditional jump-scare platforms, modern atmospheric horror relies heavily on audio design and psychological pacing. When player agency is limited—such as having a flashlight with a dying battery—the level of engagement spikes dramatically.
